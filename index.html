Questions

Good to know.

I've genuinely never exercised. Is this too much?

No, this is the set built for exactly that. The lightest band is gentle enough for a first-ever session, and every included workout can be done sitting in a chair.

Will these work if I have limited mobility?

They're designed for it. Justin's whole practice is exercise therapy for people with limited mobility, chronic conditions and joint pain. That said, if you're managing a specific condition or injury, check with your doctor or physiotherapist first.

How strong are the bands?

All six are gentle on purpose. This is a set for starting out, not heavy gym equipment. Three short bands and three long bands give you six levels to grow through at your own pace.

Will the door attachment fit my door?

Any standard door up to 7 feet (84") tall. It goes over the top of a closed door and needs no screws, hooks or tools.

How do I get the free workout videos?

There's a card in the box with a link and a QR code. Scan it with your phone camera and the five guided workouts open straight away. No app to install and nothing to pay.

Where do you ship?

Across the United States and Canada through Amazon, so you get Amazon's own delivery speed, tracking and returns. Pick your country at checkout above.

How do I clean them?

Hand wash in cool water with a little mild detergent and hang to dry. Don't put them in the tumble dryer.
